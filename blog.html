Useful questions to research before a trip

Our journal is a starting point for inspiration. Travel conditions and requirements change, so verify current details for your own dates and nationality.

Season and conditions

Compare typical weather with the activities you want, but check current forecasts and local advisories close to departure.

Culture and etiquette

Learn about local customs, respectful photography, community guidance, and how to dress appropriately at cultural or religious sites.

Documents and health

Use official sources for passport, visa, transit, vaccination, and travel-health requirements. Allow time for any required applications.

Field notes for thoughtful travel

Visiting the Maasai Mara with respect

A safari is an opportunity to experience a remarkable landscape, not a guarantee of a particular wildlife sighting. Keep a safe distance from animals, follow your guide and park rules, and avoid asking anyone to pose or share personal stories without consent.

If you are interested in a cultural visit, ask who leads it, what the experience involves, and how local participants choose to share their culture. Confirm any visit, park fees, game drives, accommodation, and transfers in your written itinerary.

Finding your pace on the Kenyan coast

A coastal stay can be as active or as restful as you want. Decide whether you prefer open beach time, water activities, food and culture, or a little of each, then leave enough space in the schedule for rest and travel connections.

Before confirming, check how you will reach your accommodation, whether activities and equipment are included, what weather conditions might affect plans, and which costs are paid locally. Follow local beach and water-safety guidance.

Making island connections easier

Island-hopping is shaped by the connections between places. Review ferry departure points, travel time, luggage limits, transfer plans, and how much time you have at each stop. Avoid tight self-booked connections unless the schedule and contingency plan are clear.

Check current entry and transit rules for your passport and route through official sources. Ask whether accommodation, ferry tickets, guided visits, local transport, meals, and taxes are included in the proposed package.

What to confirm for every package

Package summaries are a starting point. Ask for a written quote for your exact dates and party size before making travel arrangements.

Accommodation & meals

Confirm the property or accommodation standard, room sharing arrangements, meal plan, and any supplements for single occupancy.

Transport & activities

Check pickup and drop-off points, transfers between stops, included guided activities, entry fees, and any optional experiences.

Price & terms

Confirm the currency, per-person or group basis, availability, deposit and balance dates, cancellation conditions, and what is not included.

Can a package be adjusted?

Send your preferred dates, group size, interests, pace, and any must-see stops with your booking request. The team can confirm which changes are available and provide an updated quote.

Are flights and travel insurance included?

Do not assume they are included. Ask for the written itinerary to state clearly whether international or domestic flights, insurance, visas, meals, transfers, and park or attraction fees are covered.

Before booking: Displayed “from” prices are indicative. Availability and final costs can change by date, group size, and supplier confirmation.

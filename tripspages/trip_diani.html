Planning your Diani stay

Choose your pace

Leave room for unplanned beach time as well as any optional water activities. Tell us if you need a family-friendly pace or mobility support.

Pack thoughtfully

Bring sun protection, light comfortable clothing, swimwear, and any personal medication. Check current local conditions and activity-specific requirements before departure.

Confirm what is included

Ask whether accommodation, meals, transfers, water sports, boat trips, and equipment are included or optional, and confirm any extra charges.

Before you book: Confirm the exact stay dates, accommodation, transport arrangements, quote currency, and cancellation terms in writing.

Safari expectations

Game drives follow local conditions and guide advice. Wildlife sightings and any seasonal migration activity cannot be guaranteed.

Pack for the day

Bring sun protection, a warm layer for early starts, comfortable closed shoes, personal medication, and binoculars or a camera if you use them.

Confirm the quote

Ask whether park fees, shared accommodation, meals, transfers from Nairobi, and the listed game drives are included for your dates. Confirm any single-room supplement and excluded expenses.

Before travelling: Confirm pickup point and time, luggage limits, the final day-by-day schedule, payment dates, and cancellation conditions in writing.

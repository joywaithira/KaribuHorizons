What you may see

Amboseli is known for elephants and views toward Mount Kilimanjaro. Wildlife and mountain visibility depend on natural conditions and are not guaranteed.

What to bring

Pack sun protection, comfortable layers for early and late drives, closed shoes, personal medication, and any camera or binoculars you need.

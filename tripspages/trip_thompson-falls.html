Before your falls day trip

Plan for the outdoors

Wear comfortable walking shoes and bring water, sun or rain protection as appropriate, and any personal medication.

Walking routes and access can be affected by weather or local conditions. Confirm the meeting point, departure time, route, and any mobility considerations before travel.

Know what is included

Confirm transport, guide service, entry fees, meals or picnic arrangements, and any personal expenses included in the final quote.

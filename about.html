Our Story

TraveL Den Adventures began with a simple idea: travel should feel personal, not packaged. What started as a small group of Nairobi-based guides arranging weekend safaris for friends has grown into a full travel agency serving clients across Kenya and beyond. Along the way we never lost sight of why we started — to share the landscapes, culture, and craftsmanship of Africa with people who want more than a checklist of sights.

How we plan a journey

A useful itinerary starts with what matters to you. Share your dates and travel style early, then review the written details before confirming any arrangements.

Listen to your priorities

Shape the route

Review the proposed stops, travel time, activities, accommodation level, and free time. Ask for adjustments before accepting the quote.

Confirm in writing

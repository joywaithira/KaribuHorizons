Plan your trip with confidence

Whether you are choosing a Kenyan escape or an international itinerary, start with your dates, group size, preferred pace, and the experiences that matter most to you.

1. Choose an experience

Compare safari, coast, highland, cultural, and international options in the Trips menu. Each trip page includes a starting outline to help you decide.

2. Request a booking

Send your preferred trip, travel dates, number of travellers, and any accessibility or dietary requests through the booking form.

3. Confirm the details

Before you commit, ask us to confirm availability, the final price, the day-by-day itinerary, inclusions, exclusions, and payment and cancellation terms.

Good to know: Listed prices are starting prices and itinerary details can vary. A booking request is not a confirmed reservation; wait for written confirmation of the final arrangements.
Can I ask for a custom itinerary?

Yes. Include your preferred destinations, dates, group size, interests, pace, and approximate budget in your request so the team can discuss suitable options.

What should I check before paying?

Confirm what the quote includes and excludes, accommodation type, transport, meals, activities, entry fees, insurance needs, payment schedule, and cancellation terms.

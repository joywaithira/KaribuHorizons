Planning a safari

Safari pace, wildlife sightings, road conditions, and park conditions can vary. Use these notes to prepare, then confirm the details for your exact itinerary.

What to pack

Choose comfortable layers, sun protection, closed walking shoes, personal medication, and a camera or binoculars if useful. Check luggage limits and weather guidance with us.

Wildlife & conservation

Wildlife is not guaranteed. Follow your guide's safety instructions, keep a respectful distance, and never feed or approach wild animals.

Before you confirm

Ask about park fees, game-drive schedule, vehicle type, accommodation, meals, transfers, child arrangements, and any items not included in the quote.

Seasonality: Weather and wildlife patterns vary by location and year. Ask which travel window best matches your priorities; sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Packing Tips

Light layers, neutral colors, and sturdy footwear.

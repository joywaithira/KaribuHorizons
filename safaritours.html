Safari Tips & Conservation

Packing Tips

Light layers, neutral colors, and sturdy footwear.

Bring binoculars, sun protection, a compact rain jacket, and spare batteries for cameras. Respect park rules and keep safe distance from wildlife.

Conservation & Community

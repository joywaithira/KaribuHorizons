Prepare for international travel

International itineraries involve destination-specific entry rules, transport connections, and local conditions. Verify current requirements for every country on your route.

Documents & entry rules

Check passport validity, visa or electronic authorisation requirements, transit rules, and entry conditions with the relevant official authorities.

Flights & connections

Ask whether international flights, airport transfers, baggage, local transport, and connection assistance are included or need separate arrangements.

Budget & practical details

Confirm the quote currency, payment schedule, accommodation, meals, local taxes, activities, insurance needs, and any optional costs.

When should I start planning?

Start as early as your dates allow, especially when you need visas, flights, or multiple destinations. Processing times and availability vary, so verify official timelines before booking.

Will you advise on visas and health requirements?

We can help identify questions to check, but entry and health rules change. Confirm current requirements directly with official government, embassy, and health sources for your nationality and itinerary.

Important: Do not buy non-refundable flights or rely on a visa assumption until your trip dates, itinerary, and entry requirements have been independently confirmed.

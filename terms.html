Before accepting a travel quote

  • Check the exact trip dates, passenger names as required, route, accommodation, and transport arrangements.
  • Review the full price, currency, what is included and excluded, payment deadlines, and supplier cancellation rules.
  • Verify entry documents, health guidance, and insurance for every destination and transit point using official sources.
  • Ask for written clarification if an itinerary, quote, or booking condition is unclear.
